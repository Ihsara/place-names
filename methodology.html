What this maps

Every named settlement (OSM place=city/town/village/hamlet/suburb) in Finland and Vietnam, fetched via the workspace osmkit toolkit.

How a name is matched

Finland: the compound-final suffix of the single written word (longest curated suffix wins). Vietnam: the first whitespace syllable, with tone marks and đ significant (Tân ≠ Tan, Đông ≠ Dong), Unicode-normalised.

Each place gets at most one morpheme. Unmatched places appear as faint dots so density and the gaps (e.g. Khmer/Cham/montagnard naming in the Vietnamese south and highlands) stay visible. The morpheme set is curated for the story, not exhaustive. Counts are as of the build date.
